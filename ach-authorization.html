ACH Authorization

Version: web-ach-2026-08-19 · Last updated: August 19, 2026

This page shows the authorization you agree to in the mi$er app before we move any money. You complete it once, in the app, after you link your checking and savings accounts — it is separate from our Terms of Service. This page is published so you can read the terms before you start, and re-read them at any time. The copy you agree to is saved to your account with a confirmation number, and we will send you a copy.

NOTHING MOVES UNTIL YOU AUTHORIZE IT. mi$er cannot debit your account without this authorization. It covers one thing only: recurring debits from your linked checking account, to fund transfers into your own savings account. mi$er never debits your savings account.

1. The authorization statement

This is the statement you agree to in the app. Your bank name and the last four digits of your checking account are filled in with the account you linked.

I authorize MiserSavings Inc. to electronically debit my linked checking account via the ACH network for my Miser savings transfers.

This authorization is for internet-initiated (WEB) recurring debits. Questions: support@misersavings.com.

2. Amount

Variable amounts. Each debit equals the total you have locked since your last transfer, plus the service fee ($2.50 plus 1.5% of the amount saved). No single debit will exceed $500, including the fee; anything above that stays locked and goes with a later transfer. A transfer requires at least $50 locked.

Because the amount varies with what you save, you may choose to be notified of each transfer’s amount and date before it happens, or to be notified only when a transfer would fall outside the $50–$500 range. You make that choice when you authorize.

3. Schedule

Recurring, but not on a fixed day of the week. A transfer is initiated on the first day that is at least 7 days after your last completed transfer, provided you have at least $50 locked at that point.

Transfers are not initiated on a federal bank holiday. The ACH network settles on banking days only, so a transfer initiated on a weekend moves on the next banking day. If you have less than $50 locked, no transfer is taken and nothing is charged — the amount stays locked and goes with your next transfer.

Transfers settle through the ACH network: the money leaves your checking account on the debit date and typically reaches your savings account about four banking days later.

4. How to cancel or stop a transfer

You may revoke this authorization at any time, in the app under Settings → Linked banks, or by emailing support@misersavings.com. To stop a specific scheduled transfer, tell us at least 3 business days before it is scheduled.

Revocation stops future debits. It does not reverse a debit already in process. Cancelling is as easy as enrolling.

5. Your Regulation E rights

These transfers are electronic fund transfers covered by the federal Electronic Fund Transfer Act (Regulation E). You have the right to:

  • Stop a scheduled transfer by telling us at least 3 business days before its scheduled date.
  • Dispute an unauthorized or erroneous transfer. Contact us promptly — you generally must notify us within 60 days of the statement showing the transfer.
  • Request a paper copy of any disclosure.
  • Receive notice of the amount and date of a varying transfer, as described in section 2 above.

Our full Error Resolution Notice is available in the app under Settings → Legal, and is provided to you at account opening.

6. Your copy and our record

When you authorize, we save the exact wording you agreed to, the date and time, and a confirmation number to your account. You can view it in the app and save or share a copy at any time. We retain the record for as long as required by NACHA rules and applicable law.

mi$er is a financial technology company, not a bank. Both accounts in every transfer are yours, held at your own bank(s) in your name. Transfers are processed by our ACH payment partner and its partner banks, which is disclosed to you in the app at the time you authorize.
