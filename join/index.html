Someone you know is putting money toward a shared savings goal on Miser, and they want you in on it.

Miser is a savings app built on the things you decide not to buy. You log the skip, the coffee, the takeout, the thing you almost bought, and Miser adds it up. Once your pending total reaches $50, and it has been at least 7 days since your last transfer, Miser moves that money out of your checking and into your savings. Both accounts are yours, and they stay yours.

Miser is in a closed beta on iPhone. There is no public download yet, so if you do not already have the app, hold on to the code above. It is what joins you to your friend's goal once you are in.
