Frequently asked

Is my Miser total real money?

Yes. Every skip is money you chose not to spend, and once your pending total reaches $50, and it has been at least 7 days since your last transfer, it moves from your checking into your savings. Miser can't spend, keep, or lend it, it only moves between accounts you own.

How do streaks work?

Log at least one skip per calendar day to keep your streak alive. Miss a day and it resets to zero, so try to log something, even $1, every day.

Can I change or delete my goal?

Yes. Go to the Goals tab, tap "Add a new goal" to create a new one. Your Miser Savings balance carries over regardless of which goal is active.

How does my total get into savings?

Once you have $50 or more pending, and it's been at least 7 days since your last transfer, Miser bundles your skips into one ACH transfer from your checking to your savings. It lands in a few banking days. The fee is $2.50 plus 1.5% per transfer.

How does the AI coach work?

Miser AI generates personalised check-ins based on your streak, total saved, and goal progress. It's motivational only, not financial advice.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete my account." If you can't access the app, email support@misersavings.com with the subject "Delete my account."

Miser moves money between two accounts you already own at your own bank. It is not a place to keep money, so there is nothing to withdraw from Miser. There is a $500 ceiling per transaction, including the fee.

You can also add money to a goal on demand, from $50 up to $490, at the same fee, once per day.
