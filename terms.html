2. The Miser Service

Miser is a savings-habit application. When you log a skip, Miser records it in your locked total. Money moves from your linked checking account to your linked savings account, both at your bank, in your name, by ACH transfer, in two ways.

Scheduled transfers: your locked skips are bundled into a single transfer. It runs on a recurring basis but not on a fixed day of the week: it is initiated on the first day that is at least 7 days after your last scheduled transfer was initiated (including a transfer later returned by your bank), provided you have at least $50 locked at that point.

Add money: you can also start a transfer yourself from a savings goal, for any amount from $50 to $490, once per day. It is a separate transfer for the amount you enter only. It does not include your other locked skips and does not change when your next scheduled transfer runs. Miser initiates it as soon as you confirm it. If you add it after 4:00 p.m. Eastern, or on a day that is not a banking day, the bank processes it on the next banking day.

Quit Companion: if you set up a Quit Companion habit, each day you stay clean can add the daily amount you chose to your locked total. If you turn on automatic locking, Miser adds that amount each day without you confirming it, until you turn it off or report a slip. Automatic locking pauses by itself if you do not open Miser for 14 days. These amounts move with your scheduled transfers.

Transfers are not initiated on a federal bank holiday. The ACH network settles only on banking days, so a transfer initiated on a weekend moves on the next banking day, and money usually reaches your savings account about four banking days after a transfer starts. Your ACH transfers are processed by our payment partner and the regulated financial institution it works with, which hold and move the funds; Miser originates the instruction and keeps your ledger. Miser does not hold, custody, or invest your funds, and Miser is not a bank.

5. Your Locked Total

Pre-transfer: Your locked total is Miser's record of the skips you have logged that have not yet moved through a transfer. It is not money Miser holds. Until a transfer moves it, the money stays in your checking account, under your bank's terms. Your locked total is not a deposit account and is not FDIC-insured at Miser. While a transfer is in process, funds are held and moved by a regulated financial institution working with our payment partner, not by Miser. Funds in transit are held in that institution's pooled accounts and may not be covered by FDIC or NCUA insurance until they arrive in your savings account.

Post-transfer: Once your transfer clears, the dollars sit in your at-bank savings account. From that point, Miser is no longer involved in the money. It is held by your bank under your bank's terms and any FDIC coverage that applies to your account at that institution.

6. Fees

Once funds reach your linked savings account, you access them through your own bank; Miser does not charge a withdrawal fee. Miser charges a service fee on each transfer, whether scheduled or Add money: $2.50 plus 1.5% of the transfer total, with no cap. A scheduled transfer requires a minimum total of $50; if you have less than $50 locked when a transfer would otherwise run, no transfer is taken and nothing is charged. The amount stays locked and is combined into your next transfer at no extra charge. A single scheduled transfer will not debit more than $500 from your checking account, including the fee; any amount above this ceiling stays locked and carries forward to a later transfer. An Add money transfer is for $50 to $490, so its debit including the fee stays under $500. If your bank returns an Add money transfer, no money moves, no fee is charged, and nothing is re-sent; you can add it again. This service fee covers the cost of the bank transfer and the operation of the service. We will provide advance notice of at least 30 days before introducing or changing any fee. Subscriptions and overdraft charges do not exist on Miser and never will. See the in-app Error Resolution Notice (Settings → Legal) for your rights regarding unauthorized or erroneous EFTs.
