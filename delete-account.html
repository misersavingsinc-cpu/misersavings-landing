Delete your account

Two ways to do it, what goes, what stays, and how long it takes.

This page is for deleting your Miser account, run by MiserSavings Inc., and the data attached to it. You do not need the app installed and you do not need to be signed in to make the request. Nothing here costs anything, and we do not ask you to explain why.

Option 1 · In the app
1
Open the Profile tab

Then open Settings and scroll to the bottom.

2
Tap "Delete my account"

You get one confirmation screen saying exactly what will be removed. Confirm it and your account closes there and then. You are signed out and cannot sign back in.

Option 2 · By email

Use this if you have already deleted the app, cannot sign in, or would rather not do it yourself.

Send to support@misersavings.com
Subject Delete my account
Send it from The email address on the account

We reply within 24 hours on business days (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm PT) to confirm the request before anything is deleted. We confirm because an account deletion cannot be undone, and because we will not act on a request we cannot tie to the account holder. If you no longer have access to that email address, say so in the message and we will find another way to verify you.

What gets deleted

Everything that is about you:

  • Your profile and sign-in: name, email address, phone number if you gave one, profile photo.
  • Your savings record: every skip you logged, every contribution, your goals, your pinned goals and your cached totals.
  • Your streaks and ranks.
  • Quit Companion: your habits, including the category, which we treat as sensitive.
  • What the AI coach knows about you, and your coach usage history.
  • Groups: your membership, and the invitations you sent or received.
  • Your bank connections: the Plaid connection is removed, so Miser and Plaid lose access to the account, and the customer record held by our payment partner is deleted.
  • Notifications and email: your device push tokens, your notification settings, and the log of what was sent to you.
  • Your photos: profile and goal images you uploaded.
What is kept, and why

A small set of records survives, because US financial rules require it. In every one of them the link to you is severed: the record no longer identifies you, and we do not keep a key that would let us put it back.

  • ACH authorizations. When you authorize Miser to debit your checking account, that authorization is a record we are required to keep for a set period under the NACHA rules that govern ACH. It is what proves a debit was permitted.
  • Records of money that actually moved: transfers, the ledger, reconciliations, and the transfer notices sent to you under Regulation E.
  • Your acceptance of the Terms, kept in pseudonymous form: which version you accepted and when, with your identity replaced by a one-way hash.
  • Privacy requests you made, including this one, as the compliance record that we handled it.
  • Activity in shared group feeds, anonymized rather than removed, so the other members' history still adds up.
  • Aggregated, de-identified analytics. This cannot be used to identify you, we do not try to re-identify it, and we have technical safeguards against doing so.
How long it takes

Your account closes immediately. You lose access at once, and nothing further is collected about you from that moment.

The permanent removal runs 30 days later. There is one exception, and it is not discretionary: a bank can return an ACH debit up to 90 days after it was taken. If you moved money in that window, the removal waits until the window closes, because a returned debit has to be traceable to the transfer it reverses. If that applies to you, it is the only thing that makes the process take longer than 30 days.

Deleting the app does not delete your account, and it does not cancel your ACH authorization. Removing Miser from your phone leaves the account, and the authorization, exactly where they were. Use one of the two options above.
Your money is not affected

Miser never holds your money. It moves between two accounts you already own at your own bank, and Miser cannot spend, keep or lend it. Money that has already been transferred is sitting in your own savings account and stays there. Deleting your Miser account does not close, drain or touch either bank account.

If a transfer is in flight when you ask to delete, it finishes. We do not delete an account out from under money that is still moving, because that would leave a debit nobody could trace.

If you are in a group

If you are the only member of a group, the group is deleted with your account. If there are other members, the group survives and is handed to the longest-standing member, so nobody else loses a shared goal because you left.

You can also ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or clear what the AI coach knows, without deleting anything. Those rights, and the state-law rights that go with them, are in the Privacy policy. Residents of California, Texas, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have additional rights described there, and we respond to those requests within the statutory deadline.
