Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

Last reviewed September 21, 2026 · MiserSavings Inc.

Saving money should not depend on how well you see, hear, or use a mouse. We build Miser to be usable by everyone, and we would rather tell you plainly where we fall short than claim we have it all covered.

The standard we hold ourselves to

Miser aims to conform to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. That is the standard the U.S. Department of Justice adopted for public-entity websites, and it is the benchmark courts apply under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act. We apply it to misersavings.com and, as it matures, to the Miser iOS app.

Where we currently stand

This site is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially conformant means most of the site meets the standard and some parts may not yet fully do so. If you find one, tell us and we will fix it.

What we have fixed

  • Text and background colour combinations across every page now meet the 4.5:1 contrast minimum (3:1 for large text).
  • Every page has a “Skip to main content” link as its first keyboard stop, and a <main> landmark for screen reader navigation.
  • Keyboard focus is visible on every interactive element, with a 3px indicator that does not rely on the browser default.
  • Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers so they no longer interrupt reading.
  • No text on the site is smaller than 11px.
  • Animation and motion respect your operating system’s “reduce motion” setting.
  • Links that open a new window say so.
  • The waitlist form has programmatic labels, correct autofill hints, and announces its errors and confirmations to screen readers.

Tell us when we get it wrong

If any part of Miser is hard or impossible for you to use, we want to hear about it, and we would rather hear early than never. Email support@misersavings.com with what you were trying to do and what got in the way. Include the page address and the assistive technology you use, if that is relevant.

We aim to reply within 5 business days and to tell you either how to work around the problem now or when we expect to have it fixed. If you need information from this site in another format, ask and we will send it.

How we assessed this

This statement is based on a self-evaluation completed on September 21, 2026, covering all 35 published pages. The review combined automated inspection of markup and computed styles with manual keyboard testing. No third-party accessibility audit has been commissioned yet.

Compatibility

This site is designed to work with current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, together with the screen readers and zoom tools built into macOS, iOS, Windows and Android. It does not rely on JavaScript to deliver its content.

Something here not working for you?

Email support@misersavings.com. A person reads every message.
